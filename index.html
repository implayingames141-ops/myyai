What is G.E.N.?

Purpose

G.E.N. is designed as a science-focused assistant for learning, experimentation, and scientific reasoning.

Local AI

The working version runs locally through Ollama, so the core model does not require an online AI API.

Project Goal

The science-fair project investigates G.E.N.'s accuracy and response time on standardized science questions and compares those measurements with human-student results.

What G.E.N. Is Designed to Do

🔬 Concepts

Explain scientific concepts clearly at a student-friendly level.

🧪 Experiments

Help identify independent, dependent, and controlled variables.

📊 Data

Help interpret tables, graphs, observations, and experimental data.

🧮 Calculations

Work through scientific calculations and explain the reasoning.

🌎 Science Areas

Support questions in biology, chemistry, physics, Earth & space science, and scientific method/data analysis.

⚠️ Uncertainty

G.E.N. is instructed not to invent evidence, measurements, sources, or experimental results and to acknowledge uncertainty.

The Science-Fair Investigation

Research Question

How does G.E.N.'s accuracy and response time on standardized science questions compare with that of human students?


30 Questions

6 Biology
6 Chemistry
6 Physics
6 Earth & Space Science
6 Scientific Method / Data Analysis

Measurements

Accuracy is the primary measurement. Response time, explanation quality/completeness, and whether uncertainty is appropriately acknowledged can also be recorded.

Fair Testing

Use the same standardized questions and a predefined scoring method. Keep participant information to the minimum needed and follow school/science-fair rules.


Response time should be treated as a separate measurement because typing and reading time can make human and AI response times different in meaning.

Limitations & Responsible Use

AI Can Be Wrong

A fluent answer is not automatically a correct answer. Scientific claims should be checked against reliable sources when accuracy matters.

Not a Source of Experimental Evidence

G.E.N. should not be treated as evidence that an experiment produced a particular result.

Hardware Matters

The current Qwen3 4B setup is intended for the Windows PC where it has been tested. A USB drive provides storage but does not add the RAM or computing power required to run the model.

Safety First

Real experiments should follow appropriate safety procedures, supervision, and science-fair rules. G.E.N. is an educational tool, not a replacement for a teacher or qualified professional.

Project Summary

G.E.N. — Generalized Experimental Navigator — combines a Python interface, the Ollama local AI runtime, and the Qwen3 4B language model into a student-built science assistant. The project focuses not only on making an AI answer questions, but on measuring its performance scientifically using standardized questions, predefined scoring, and recorded response times.
